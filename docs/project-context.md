## Rubric (keep using this)

Judge only the **[0–3s]** beat (first spoken line, on-screen text, or first image beat the prompt specifies).

Strong hook: specific; curiosity gap or tension; names a concrete pain or payoff; starts immediately.

Weak hook: greeting, slow setup, scene-only, generic insult/eviction (“get out”, “pack your things”, “this is my house”) with no irreplaceable detail (money, keys, room, date, object).

## Recurring weak patterns found

1. Formula eviction/insult openings with no unique stake.
2. Sharp line delayed past 3s, or first 3s are only atmosphere (enter room, chop tomatoes, airport wait, greeting).
3. Duplicate opening lines across many rows (e.g. “She's just the maid!”, “You watched me drown!”) — fine alone, weak if posted back-to-back.
